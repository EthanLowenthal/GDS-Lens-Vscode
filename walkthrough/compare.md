## Compare two layouts

Two ways to start:

- With a layout open, press `Ctrl+K D` (`Cmd+K D` on macOS) or click the
  compare button in the editor title bar, then pick the other file.
- Select two layout files in the Explorer, right-click, and choose
  **Compare Layouts**.

Both layouts are drawn in one viewer. In the **Compare** folder:

- **A ↔ B** crossfades between them.
- **Tint sources** gives each layout its own hue.
- **Highlight differences** marks where they disagree: red where only A has
  geometry, green where only B does.
