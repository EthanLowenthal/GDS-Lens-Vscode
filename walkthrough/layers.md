## Layer colors

Every layer gets a color with no setup. To use your PDK's colors and layer
names, open **Display** in the panel and click **Load .lyp File**. The `.lyp`
is applied to every layout you open after that, until you unload it.

In the **Layers** list:

| Control | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Toggle | Show or hide the layer |
| **S** | Show only this layer; click again to restore |
| Filter box | Match by number, datatype, name or group |
| **Show: All \| None \| Invert** | Applies to the filtered rows |
